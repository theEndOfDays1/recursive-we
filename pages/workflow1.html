Detailed Guide on Price of Website Design in Nigeria.

Up-to-date breakdown of what it will cost you to own a website in 2026. Click a category on the left to inspect its complete pricing breakdown.

Domain Name Registration

This is your website's unique address on the internet, with extensions such as .com, .net, .org, etc.

Current 2026 Estimate: N40,000+ per year for international domains (pegged around NGN1400/$). Local .ng domains range between N15,000 – N25,000.

Pro Strategy: If your ideal .com name is taken, opt for a .com.ng or .ng extension just like top startups do, saving you from high domain broker backorder fees.

Web Hosting Infrastructure

Hosting comes in 4 basic categories depending on your monthly traffic volume and server demands:

  • Shared Hosting: $60 – $168 (N90,000 – N252,000) per year. Ideal for standard business sites.
  • VPS / Cloud Hosting: $360 – $1700 (N540k – N2.55M+ per year). Recommended for high-traffic blogs or large organizations.
  • Dedicated Server: $1600 – $4200 (N2.4M+ – N6.3M+ per year) for heavy enterprise-scale platforms.

SSL Security Certificate

SSL encryption is now a strict requirement for all modern browsers and search engines. Sites lacking an SSL certificate are flagged as "Insecure".

Why it matters: Operating without an SSL certificate deals an immediate, severe dent to your company's digital reputation and trust.

Web Developer’s Professional Fee

Determined by project scope—whether static, dynamic (CMS), or full eCommerce.

  • Static 5-Page Site: Ranges from N70,000 to N350,000+ depending on developer experience.
  • Dynamic & CMS: Varies from N70k to millions depending on bespoke requirements vs. template efficiency.
  • E-Commerce: Scaled by product catalog size and payment gateway integrations (e.g., Paystack, Flutterwave).

Minimum Total Budget Estimate: NGN325,000 (Excl. SSL) to NGN365,000 (Incl. SSL).
