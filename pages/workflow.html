Frequently asked questions.

A few things you might want to know before getting started.

How do I start a project?

Use our contact form to tell us about your project. We'll review your requirements and get in touch to discuss the next steps.

Do I have to pay everything upfront?

Payment arrangements depend on the service and project scope. The agreed payment terms will be communicated before work begins.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. Revision allowances depend on the service package and the original project agreement. Additional features or substantial changes may require a separate quote.

Will I own my website and source code?

Ownership and transfer arrangements will be outlined in your project agreement. Third-party assets, libraries, and licenses remain subject to their respective terms.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Maintenance and continued development can be discussed as separate services after the initial project delivery.

Can I hire you through an external platform?

Yes. Where available, you can engage with us through supported freelance platforms. The relevant platform's terms and payment arrangements will apply.
